An idealized model, not a hardness assumption: the adversary is assumed to treat
group elements as opaque handles. Proofs here rule out generic attacks only.

Wagner's list asks for *established* assumptions and flags idealizations
explicitly. [[gkpw-silent-setup]] and [[beast-mev]] are both GGM proofs, which is
worth knowing when comparing them against a standard-model scheme.
