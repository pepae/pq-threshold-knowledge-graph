A family of pairing assumptions parameterised by a query count q, so the
assumption itself grows with the scheme's usage. Weaker footing than a static
assumption like [[ddh]], and not post-quantum.

Used by [[waters-wu-silent]] in the standard model, and by the [[btx]] line.
