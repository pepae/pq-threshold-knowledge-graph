A simple and falsifiable lattice assumption used by
[[champion-wu-monotone-dnf]] and [[champion-wu-optimal-dnf]] for distributed
monotone-policy encryption, and by [[rishab-dme]] in an l-decomposed form.

This is currently the most promising route to post-quantum silent setup, so it is
the assumption to watch for [[D2]] and [[D8]] jointly.
