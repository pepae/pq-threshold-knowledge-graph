Lattice-based zero-knowledge proofs, shorter, simpler and more general than
prior work (CRYPTO 2022, eprint 2022/284). The practical default for proving
well-formedness of a lattice ciphertext, which is what a batched scheme needs per
ciphertext for [[D7]].
