## What it asks

Each decryptor contributes exactly one message, computed from its own key share
and the ciphertext. No decryptor-to-decryptor communication, no rounds.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Decryption sits on the block critical path. Every extra round is a consensus
round, and a party that must speak twice is a party that can stall the chain by
speaking once.

## How schemes fail it

Two-round protocols appear whenever a lattice trapdoor must be applied jointly.
[[blt-batch-ibe]] needs a two-round protocol for secret-shared Gaussian preimage
sampling, which is the price of its succinct pre-decryption key. Epoch-based
schemes like [[cgpp-bte]] push the interaction into a per-epoch MPC setup rather
than into decryption itself, which satisfies the letter of this desideratum while
relocating the cost; see [[D14]].
