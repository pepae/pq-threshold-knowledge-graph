## What it asks

The adversary chooses which parties to corrupt during the protocol, informed by
what it has already seen, rather than fixing the corrupt set before setup.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Wagner grades this one as *both seems fine, adaptive preferred*, which makes it
the most relaxable item on the list. Static corruption is a genuine modelling gap
but not a deployment blocker.

## State of the art

Standard-model adaptive CCA2 without pairings was achieved by
[[devevey-libert-nguyen-peters-yung]] from DCR and LWE, but at superpolynomial
modulus for the lattice instantiation.
[[sjtu-adaptive-threshold-decryption]] closes that at polynomial modulus.
[[hall-andersen-simkin-wagner-silent]] introduces *one-shot* adaptive
corruptions, a model strictly between static and fully adaptive.
Adaptive corruptions for *batched* lattice schemes remain open; see
[[adaptive-corruptions-batched-lattice]].
