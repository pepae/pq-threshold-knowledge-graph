## What it asks

Small public keys. Under silent setup the relevant measure is the *individual*
posted key, since those are what the PKI must carry and what every encryptor must
fetch.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Every user encrypting a transaction needs the encryption key. If it scales with
committee size, wallets pay for decentralisation on every send.

## Where schemes stand

[[gkpw-silent-setup]] has linear-size individual public keys.
[[hall-andersen-simkin-wagner-silent]] has small individual keys but larger
ciphertexts, an explicit trade. [[beat-mev]] has very large keys.
Post-quantum is worst: 259 MB measured for the PQ BEAT-MEV instantiation at
batch 512, per [[beatmev-pq-implementation]].
