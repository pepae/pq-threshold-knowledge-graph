## What it asks

Security against a quantum adversary, resting on assumptions that plausibly
survive one.

## Mapping note

Wagner's list has a separate bullet, *hardness assumptions and idealizations (want
to use established ones)*, which this D1-D15 numbering does not give its own
number. Treat assumption quality as part of the evidence for D8 rather than as a
missing desideratum, and read it off the `assumes` edges: a scheme on
[[module-lwe]] and a scheme on a q-type pairing assumption in the generic group
model are not equally well founded even when both are labelled with the same
status. See `vault/desiderata/_wagner-mapping.md`.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Encrypted mempool ciphertexts are only secret for one block, so a store-now
decrypt-later adversary gains little. The real exposure is the committee's
long-lived key material, which does not rotate per block.

## State of the art

Every pairing-based scheme fails this outright. The lattice route works but is
expensive: measured overhead of three to four orders of magnitude at batch 512,
per [[beatmev-pq-implementation]].
