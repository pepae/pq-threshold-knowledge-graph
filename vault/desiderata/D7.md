## What it asks

Batched-specific. An adversary who injects malformed or maliciously related
ciphertexts into a batch must not learn anything about honest ciphertexts in that
batch, nor about ciphertexts outside it.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Batching creates a shared fate: one short decryption key opens many ciphertexts.
An attacker who can place a chosen ciphertext into the same batch as a victim's
gets a free oracle query unless the scheme defends against it. This is not
hypothetical; it is the class of attack [[choudhuri-garg-piet-policharla-2024]]
documents.

## How it is achieved

A well-formedness proof attached to each ciphertext, checked before the ciphertext
is admitted to a batch. [[beat-mev]] needs straight-line simulation-extractable
NIZKs for exactly this, efficient in the algebraic group model.
