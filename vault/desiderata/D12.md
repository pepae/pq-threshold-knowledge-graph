## What it asks

The value the committee publishes to open a block should be short and independent
of what it opens.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

The decryption key is broadcast on chain and every node must download it. This is
the quantity that decides whether decryption scales with block size.

## The two sub-bullets

Wagner attaches two caveats. First, hybrid encryption gets you short keys but
must be done carefully, which is the associated-data point tracked under [[D4]].
Second, *threshold IBE solutions are ideal for that setting*, because an IBE
pre-decryption key for a set of identities is exactly a short opener for a batch.
That is the route [[blt-batch-ibe]] takes.
