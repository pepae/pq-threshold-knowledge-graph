## What it asks

Small per-party partial decryptions.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Shares are the committee's per-block bandwidth. With a large committee this is
the dominant communication cost, and unlike ciphertext size it is paid by
infrastructure rather than users.

## Where schemes stand

The batched pairing schemes are excellent here and deliberately so: 80 bytes per
party for an entire batch in [[cgpp-bte]], 48 bytes in
[[choudhuri-garg-policharla-wang-onetime]], both independent of batch size.
[[pilvi]] reports 1 to 4 KB with shares of size t log K poly(lambda).
The lattice batched route hits a wall from two sides:
[[linear-route-opening-impossibility]] rules out the cheap construction, and
[[blt25-implementation]] shows the succinct one does not run at secure parameters.
See [[sublinear-pq-batch-decryption]]. Note also that the succinctness/sub-batch
trade-off carries its own cost in [[index-collision-censorship]], so smaller shares
are not free even when achievable.
