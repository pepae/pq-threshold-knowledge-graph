## What it asks

Low ciphertext overhead relative to the plaintext transaction.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Ciphertexts go on chain. Overhead is paid in blockspace by every user, forever,
and it competes directly with the throughput the chain is trying to protect.

## Where schemes stand

Pairing-based schemes are in the hundreds of bytes: 722 B for [[beat-mev]] at
batch 512. Lattice schemes are FrodoKEM-scale at best, and the measured PQ
BEAT-MEV ciphertext is 1.9 MB, roughly 2700x its classical counterpart.
[[pilvi]] reports 14 to 58 KB. This is the clearest single statement of the
lattice tax.
