## What it asks

Two things at once. First, batch decryption: one short decryption key opens a
whole block of ciphertexts, with communication independent of or sublinear in the
batch size, and without compromising ciphertexts that were *not* included.
Second, no epochs: no per-batch setup, no slot number a user must commit to at
encryption time.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

The epoch-free half is what makes the primitive deployable. An epoch-based scheme
needs a fresh, often MPC-based, setup per batch, and forces users to encrypt to a
specific future slot. Users do not know which block will include them.

## Where schemes stand

[[cgpp-bte]] introduced batched threshold encryption but is epoch-based with a
costly per-batch MPC setup. [[choudhuri-garg-policharla-wang-onetime]] removes
the per-epoch setup but stays epoch-based. [[beat-mev]] is the first epoch-free
construction. [[blt-batch-ibe]] is epoch-free via threshold batch IBE.

## Why this is the query that matters

Filtering for schemes that are both post-quantum and epoch-free-batched is the
sharpest way to see the open frontier:

```
python3 skill/scripts/query.py filter --type scheme --pq true --satisfies D14
```
