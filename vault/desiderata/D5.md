## What it asks

The adversary may choose its challenge ciphertext after seeing public keys and
after querying the decryption oracle, rather than committing to it up front.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Selective security models an attacker who picks a target transaction before
seeing the chain state. Real front-runners do the opposite: they watch, then
choose.

## How schemes fail it

Lattice schemes with succinct components are the usual offenders.
[[champion-wu-monotone-dnf]] is (semi-)selective; the plain-model fully succinct
variant in [[champion-wu-optimal-dnf]] is explicitly selective only. Straight-line
simulation-extractable NIZKs are the standard route to adaptivity, at the cost of
an a priori query bound; see [[remove-query-bound-poly-modulus]].
