## What it asks

CCA2 security, and, when the scheme is used as a KEM under hybrid encryption, a
threshold KEM that binds associated data. Wagner flags the second half explicitly:
without associated-data binding, the hybrid construction does not preserve CCA2
even if the KEM is CCA2 on its own.

## Why it matters for encrypted mempools

Transactions are attacker-chosen and the decryption oracle is a public service:
the committee will decrypt whatever lands in a block. That is a CCA2 setting by
construction, not a theoretical nicety.

## How schemes fail it

Many concretely efficient constructions are proven CPA and left there.
[[beast-mev]] is CCA-secure but its most efficient reported variant is the CPA
one. Getting from CPA to CCA generically is the subject of
[[brzuska-klooss-woo]], which supplies a Naor-Yung style transform needing
semi-malicious CPA security and a random-oracle transform built on a new
non-interactive proof of randomness.
