- **Circuit ABE and succinct LWE (l-succinct LWE)** - Wee (CRYPTO 2024)
  - no verified identifier yet; do not cite one until confirmed
  - The origin of the l-succinct LWE assumption that Champion-Wu and TACET's silent variant depend on. The assumption node currently cites it second-hand. eprint number not yet verified, so do not cite one.
- **Key Homomorphic PRFs and Their Applications** - Boneh, Lewi, Montgomery, Raghunathan (CRYPTO 2013)
  - no verified identifier yet; do not cite one until confirmed
  - The BLMR matrix-product PRF that beatmev-pq-implementation instantiates. Needed to check the PQ BEAT-MEV parameter derivation.
- **EIP-8105: Universal Enshrined Encrypted Mempool**
  - [https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8105](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8105)
  - The deployment target. Encryption-agnostic by design, so it defines the interface any scheme in this KB would have to meet. Not a paper, but the most decision-relevant document outside the literature.
- **SoK: Consensus for Fair Message Ordering**
  - [https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09981)
  - Post-inclusion ordering is an open problem in this KB and is a consensus question, not a cryptography one. This is the survey that frames it.
