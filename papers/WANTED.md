Generated by `scripts/check_pdfs.py --write`. Do not edit by hand: add or
remove entries in `papers/wanted.yml` and regenerate. Anything whose PDF
lands in `papers/pdf/` drops off this list automatically.

Save each file as its eprint id with the slash replaced by a hyphen, for
example `2024-669.pdf`. Prefer the eprint version over a publisher PDF:
eprint submissions are CC BY or author-retained, publisher PDFs are not.

## Curated wishlist

### Priority 1: the KB makes a claim it cannot fully source

- **Mempool Privacy via Batched Threshold Encryption: Attacks and Defenses** - Choudhuri, Garg, Piet, Policharla
  - `2024/669` -> save as `2024-669.pdf` - [pdf](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/669.pdf)
  - The paper that introduces batched threshold encryption and the attacks motivating D7. Several notes cite its attacks; none can quote them. USENIX Security 2024.
- **Silent Threshold Encryption with One-Shot Adaptive Security** - Hall-Andersen, Simkin, Wagner
  - `2025/1384` -> save as `2025-1384.pdf` - [pdf](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1384.pdf)
  - The one-shot adaptive corruption model sits between static and fully adaptive and is cited in D6. Also the paper most often misread as the folklore naive baseline, so the KB needs its actual construction on record.
- **Weighted Batched Threshold Encryption with Applications to Mempool Privacy** - Agarwal, Babel, Das, Poorebrahim Gilkalaye, Mondal, Pinkas, Rindal, Yadav
  - `2025/2115` -> save as `2025-2115.pdf` - [pdf](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/2115.pdf)
  - The FFT-friendly replacement for BEAT-MEV's KH-PPRF, and the only weighted construction in the KB. Stake-weighted committees are the realistic deployment shape on a PoS chain.
- **Practical Mempool Privacy via One-time Setup Batched Threshold Encryption** - Choudhuri, Garg, Policharla, Wang
  - `2024/1516` -> save as `2024-1516.pdf` - [pdf](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/1516.pdf)
  - The middle step between epoch-based and epoch-free, and per Wagner it needs a new interactive assumption for its proof. That assumption node cannot be written honestly without the paper.
